## **JUDGE QUESTIONS & ANSWERS**

**Q: How do you ensure data privacy?**
A: We use anonymization and zero-knowledge proofs. No personal data stored.

**Q: What about false positives?**
A: Multi-layer validation with 94% accuracy, and human-in-the-loop for critical alerts.

**Q: How do you handle different languages?**
A: Multilingual BERT models trained on Indian financial text.

**Q: Business model?**
A: SaaS for regulators ($50K/year), enterprise for banks ($100K/year).

**Q: What's next?**
A: Expand to SE Asia, add voice analysis from customer calls, blockchain evidence ledger.
